# Sales-Performance-Analysis
📊 Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI.
The dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of business sales performance across multiple dimensions, including time periods, customer segments, countries, and products.
It transforms sales data into meaningful visual insights, helping business stakeholders monitor KPIs, understand profitability, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

🎯 Project Objectives

Monitor overall business sales performance.
Analyse revenue and profitability trends.
Compare sales performance across customer segments.
Identify top-performing countries and products.
Evaluate discounts and their impact on net sales.
Analyse Cost of Goods Sold (COGS).
Track profit margins and business growth.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Microsoft Power BI – Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
Power BI Visualizations – KPI cards, bar charts, line charts, and data tables.
Business Intelligence – Performance monitoring and business reporting.

💡 Key Business Insights

Government is the highest-performing customer segment by sales volume and net sales.
Small Business is another significant contributor to overall revenue.
France leads the countries in total profit.
Paseo is the highest-performing product in terms of units sold, net sales, and profit.
Enterprise shows negative profitability, highlighting an area that requires further investigation.
Overall business profit margin stands at approximately 14%.
